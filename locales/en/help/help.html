Destination
The media file, as named in the media manager. It is delivered by its alias without showing its real address.
Alias
The end of the public address of the file, after the pub/ shown in the column title. The link opens it.
Password
If set, visitors must enter it to get the file. It is kept as written and shown in this list.
Disposable
A disposable alias deletes itself after the file has been delivered once.
Update
Saves every change made in the list.
